# Night-of one-pager — club label printing

Print this page and tape it near the check-in laptop. Setup is in [SETUP.md](SETUP.md); problems are in [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

Since KVBC Awana 10.0.0 there is no Chrome extension and no widget on the
TwoTimTwo page. Check-in is **awana.kvbchurch.org/checkin**; the laptop's
KVBC Awana prints the labels the check-in service sends it.

## Before doors open (5 minutes)

1. **Start the app** — KVBC Awana starts with Windows; if its tray icon
   (near the clock) is missing, double-click **KVBC Awana** on the desktop.
2. **Open the check-in page** — **awana.kvbchurch.org/checkin** in any
   browser at the desk, signed in with the church passphrase. The list of
   families appears (the first read after a quiet week can take up to a
   minute: "Signing in to TwoTimTwo…").
3. **Print a test label** — awana.kvbchurch.org/settings → Printing → At
   the laptop → *Print a test label*. A label with a big **TEST** band comes
   out.
4. **Glance at the settings page** (SETUP.md §3), in Chrome on this
   laptop — At the laptop should say *Everything looks right*.

## During check-in

- Labels print by themselves, within seconds, when a child is checked in at
  /checkin (the desk or a volunteer's phone) or on TwoTimTwo's own check-in
  page.
- **Label didn't print?** The settings page → Printing → *Tonight's
  prints* (the passphrase opens the names) → **Reprint**.
- **Kid not in the system?** /checkin → **+** → *Walk-in name tag*: type
  the first name, pick a club, print. They are greeted on the screens and
  never counted.
- **Phone check-in:** volunteers open **awana.kvbchurch.org/checkin** on
  their phone (any network, church passphrase to sign in) and tap the kid.
  The label prints here.
- **Leader name tag:** /checkin → **+** → *Leader tag*, or the settings
  page's *Name tags and labels* card. Never counts as a kid. Leaders you
  printed before come back as chips; the **×** forgets one.
- **CHECK THE PRINTER** on the tray or the settings page: a label went to the
  printer and the app could not tell whether it came out. Look at the
  printer, reprint if it is missing, then press *I looked at it*.

## Paper sheet when the internet is down

Nothing checks in or prints without the internet: /checkin, TwoTimTwo and
the print jobs all go through it. Keep this kit at the desk every week:

- **TwoTimTwo's paper sign-in sheet**, printed beforehand while online:
  TwoTimTwo's Print Form page (`/clubber/checkin_form`, grouped by club),
  for the coming club night.
- **Blank name tags** (or blank labels) and a **marker**.

The drill:

1. The moment /checkin cannot reach the service (it says so, or the
   list stops loading), switch the desk to paper. Don't wait on it.
2. Each child: tick them on their club's sheet and write their first name
   and club on a blank tag. Ask the parent about allergies and write them on
   the tag too: with no internet the labels' allergy icons are not there.
3. Walk-ins: write them at the bottom of the club's sheet with a parent's
   phone number.
4. When the internet is back, key the sheet into TwoTimTwo: its Form
   Entry page (`/clubber/checkin_form_entry`, pick the night and the club),
   or /checkin while it is still club night (labels then print for those
   children; skip the ones already wearing a hand-written tag).

## If printing stops

1. Is the tray icon there? If not, double-click **KVBC Awana** again —
   jobs the service is holding print once it is back (each at most once).
2. Printer on, paper in, USB seated? The app retries by itself, and a
   backup printer (if one is set) takes over.
3. The settings page (SETUP.md §3, in Chrome on this laptop) shows exactly
   what's wrong — At the laptop (warnings, *Run diagnostics*) and *Print
   failures tonight*. With no internet use the app's status window (tray →
   *Print server status...*). Then
   [TROUBLESHOOTING.md](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

**The screen never blocks the door.** Worst case: the paper sheet above.
