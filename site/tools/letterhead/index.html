How the text is laid out
  • The first line is the title; the first paragraph is the lead.
  • Every other paragraph (or a line starting - ) is a reminder with a sparkle bullet.
  • A short line with no period is a section heading (or start a line with ## ).
  • 11/4 - text or Nov 4 - text is an event with a date chip.
  • A paragraph after a section's events is a callout box.
  • The last 2 to 5 short lines are the signature.
  • Web and email addresses become links. Long text shrinks to stay on one page.
