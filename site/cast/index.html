Show something

  • Join the KVBC_Guest Wi-Fi first. Your browser asks which screen, window or tab. To share sound too, pick a tab and tick “Share tab audio”.

  • iPhones and iPads cannot share their screen from a web page. Everything below still works.

    • or paste a link

      A link with a start time (…?t=90) starts there.

    • On the video, tap Share, then Copy link. Only public videos play; a private group’s or a friends-only post won’t.

    • MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 200 MB. A big video takes a few minutes to send; keep this page open until it plays.

    • or

      A PowerPoint takes about a minute to turn into slides; a PDF goes up at once. Google Slides must be shared as “Anyone with the link”. Animations and videos inside slides don’t carry over; for those, use Share my screen.

    Anything you put up is cleared when Cast is turned off.
