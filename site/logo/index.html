KVBC Awana logo

The logo of Kennebec Valley Baptist Church Awana Clubs, for anything made for the clubs: screens, bulletins, flyers, shirts and slides. Three versions, always one solid color, with the color chosen by the background. Download the version you need below.

Choosing a version. Use the full logo where the tagline can be read. Use two-line in smaller spaces and stacked in tall, narrow ones. All three share the same lettering and line spacing.

Color

One color, picked by the background

The logo is never two colors and never gets an outline, shadow or gradient. White on anything colored or dark, ink on anything light.

Motion

Arrives once, then stays still

When a screen loads, the church line lands, then Awana Clubs, then the tagline, with the screens' soft squish. After that it never moves. With reduced motion turned on it simply appears.

On the projectorAbout 1.2 seconds, once.
Size

Smallest sizes

"BAPTIST CHURCH" is a thin weight, so the church line goes first when the logo gets small. Below these widths, pick the next version down.

Rules

Keeping it from looking forced

Do

Leave clear space around it equal to the height of the "KENNEBEC VALLEY" line.

Don't

Stretch or squash it to fill a space. Use another version instead.

Don't

Color one part differently. The whole logo is one color.

Don't

Add shadows, outlines or effects. The screens' hard shadows are for headlines, not the logo.

Don't

Squeeze the full version into a small corner. If the tagline can't be read, use two-line.

Don't

Use ink on a strong color. Strong colors and dark backgrounds take white.
