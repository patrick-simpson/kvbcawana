Nothing to set here: this screen runs by the clock on club nights.

  • 6:30 the Cubbies card
  • About 7:00 black for a few minutes, while the clubs pass at the door
  • Then Sparks Large Group Time
  • 7:30 the lobby screen, Sparks only
  • 8:00 this computer shuts down (the shutdown setting on the left)

A Cubbies or Sparks child who checks in while their card is up gets a welcome and a ding.

Lifting a SMART Board pen (or touching the board with one, or the small pen button in the bottom left) turns the screen black to write on until the next scheduled screen. Set SMART Ink's black pen to write white (SMART Settings, Pen and Button Settings).
